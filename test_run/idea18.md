**Key Features:**

1. **Interactive Learning Modules:** Users navigate through levels that teach them about personal finance, investment strategies, and market fundamentals. Each level has mini-games and challenges, such as a stock-picking competition or a budgeting puzzle.

3. **Social Investing:** Users can form guilds where they collaboratively manage a virtual portfolio, encouraging teamwork and shared knowledge. The real-time performance of their virtual investments could teach users about risk management and diversification.

4. **Expert Challenges:** Monthly competitions hosted by finance experts where users can submit investment strategies or predictions for prizes. This not only engages users but also provides them the opportunity to learn from industry leaders.

5. **Augmented Reality Experiences:** Imagine using AR to visualize stock market trends in your living room or engage with digital avatars of historical financial figures to learn about their strategies!
